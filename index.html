Frequently asked questions

The key information about our process, collaboration and outcomes before you start a
project.

How long does it take to prepare a new website?

A standard project usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. The timeline depends on the scope, number
of pages and how quickly the materials are provided.

Can you help prepare the copy and images?

Yes. We can organise existing materials, prepare the content structure and recommend a
direction for imagery and graphics that fits the brand.

Will the website work well on mobile?

Yes. We design for phones, tablets and desktops from the start. Every key view is checked
across several screen sizes.

Can I edit the website after launch?

Yes. We structure the project so the most important content can be updated easily. We also
provide a short handover guide after launch.
